Cancellation and Refund Policy

Effective date: [date]

How to cancel

Open your account page in Mr.MCQuuu and choose "Cancel subscription". You can cancel at any time and there is no cancellation fee. If you cannot sign in, email [support email] from the address on your account.

What happens when you cancel

  • No further payments are taken.
  • You keep Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • After that your account moves to the Free plan. Question sets already saved in your browser stay available.

Refunds

Because Pro gives immediate access to a digital service, payments are generally not refundable and we do not refund partly used periods. We will refund a payment in full if:

  • you were charged more than once for the same period;
  • you were charged after you had cancelled; or
  • a fault on our side meant you could not use Pro at all during the period, and we could not fix it within a reasonable time.

To ask for a refund, email [support email] within 14 days of the charge with the email address on your account and the date of the payment. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method, usually within 7 to 14 working days depending on your bank or e-wallet.

One-off passes

If you bought access for a fixed period with a one-off payment, it ends automatically and there is nothing to cancel. The refund rules above apply in the same way.

Your legal rights

This policy does not limit any right you have under consumer protection law.
